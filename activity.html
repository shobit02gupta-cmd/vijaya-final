Our Activities

  • Participation in various reliefs, rehabilitation, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) programme
  • Operation of mobile medical health services.
  • Arranging of issue based workshops, seminars etc
  • Undertaking ISD, Induction, Sensitization programmes like training of Medical Officers and Para-Medical personnel under RCH programmes.
  • Managing Special School of Blind, Deaf & Dumb Girls.
  • Managing SWADHAR Homes accommodating and taking care of distressed, disadvantaged and deserted widows and women
  • Undertaking preparation of consumable items like curd, chhatua, wicks, badi and serving activities like supply of daily OMFED MILK through our distribution booth and grinding of wheat, chilly, turmeric and other spices.
  • Undertaking surveys, field studies.
  • Conducting awareness training for HIV/AIDS and nutrition and educational programmes for tribal population.
  • Undertaking Catering Services
  • Undertaking translation of Trauma Counseling Modules developed by American Red Cross (New Delhi) and Asha Training Modules, SAB Training Modules etc into Oriya.
  • Undertaking programmes for nutrition and educational achievement for Chikutia Bhunjia Tribal people in Nuapada District.

About Us

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th October 1996 bearing Registration No- "20154/118-1996-97". The organization has been registered under Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act 1976 and also registered under u/s 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income Tax Act 1961.
